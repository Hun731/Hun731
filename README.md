# Krishna Kant

Computational protein, enzyme, and materials design. I generate molecular systems, simulate them, and
score them on physics before making a claim. Most of what I build runs end to end on an in-house GPU
workstation and on national HPC.

Dual degree in Bioscience and Biotechnology, IIT Kharagpur. Founder, Quiescent Labs.

### What I work on

- **De novo protein and enzyme design** over RFdiffusion3, LigandMPNN, AlphaFold3, and Boltz, gated on
  geometry and quantum chemistry rather than on fold alone.
- **Molecular dynamics** in GROMACS, all-atom and coarse-grained, with free-energy analysis and a habit
  of validating pipelines before trusting them.
- **Quantum chemistry** at scale in ORCA: DFT and TD-DFT for photophysics, metal-site coordination
  libraries, and force-constant derivation.
- **Machine learning for molecules**: property prediction with DFT validation in the loop, reaction
  prediction, and active-learning search.

### Public tools

- **[polymer-engine](https://github.com/Hun731/polymer-engine)** — orchestration engine for polymer
  discovery over MD and QM, with provenance and active learning.
- **[orca-tools](https://github.com/Hun731/orca-tools)** — ORCA output parsing, Seminario force
  constants, and job-status triage.
